2.5.0 (build 12)

A new pitch detector to try: "pYIN + note model". It holds a sustained note steadier than the default YIN: on recorded guitar, 89% of held-note readings were right against 81%. The first reading after a pluck comes about 30 ms later, and between strummed chords it shows a reading more often. YIN stays the default.

What to test

• In settings, switch the pitch detector to "pYIN + note model" and tune a few strings. Does the needle stay calmer while you turn a peg?
• Pluck a string and watch how quickly the first reading appears, compared with YIN.
• Strum a chord, then let it ring out. Does the tuner show readings you would not expect?
• Switch back to YIN and check that tuning works as before.
