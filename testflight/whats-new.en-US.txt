CrispTuner 2.5.0 — the extra transcription models now work.

Tuning itself is unchanged. What is new: the four extra models in the model menu (MT3, Piano transcription, Onsets & Frames, hFT-Transformer) were listed before but could not run. This build includes the engine they need.

Please try:

1. Stop listening first (the menu is greyed out while the microphone is on). In the settings panel, open the menu with the piano icon and pick a model. Start with hFT-Transformer: it is the smallest (4.5 MB).
2. The app asks before downloading and says how big the file is. Tap Download and watch the progress bar. Try Cancel once too.
3. Start listening, switch on "Detect chords" under Chord transcription, and play a chord or a short phrase. The notes should appear.
4. MT3 (96 MB) is the most accurate on real music and names the instrument. Best tried on Wi-Fi.
5. "Remove downloaded model" under the menu should delete it and go back to the built-in model.

What we would most like to hear:
• Whether the notes keep up with your playing, and on which device. This build is the first time these models run on a phone, tablet or Mac, so their speed there is unknown.
• Anything that seems frozen during a download or while transcribing.
• On a Mac older than macOS 13.3 the extra models should show as unavailable, and everything else should work as before.

Privacy: downloading a model is the only time the app connects to the internet. The file comes from huggingface.co, once, and stays on the device. No audio or settings are sent.
