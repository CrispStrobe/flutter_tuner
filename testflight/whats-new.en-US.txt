2.5.0 (build 13)

The buttons in the top-right corner, reworked.

• The record button is bigger, and the settings button now sits to its right.
• The settings button opens the settings in a panel over the tuner, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. It used to scroll down to the settings, which did nothing when they were already on screen.

Also in 2.5.0, unchanged since build 12: the "pYIN + note model" pitch detector to try, and the four extra transcription models that now run on the device.

What to test

• Start and stop tuning with the big record button.
• Tap the gear: the settings should open over the tuner. Change the A4 pitch or the instrument and check that the change shows at once, there and in the tuner.
• Close the panel by swiping it down or tapping outside it, then start tuning again.
• On iPad and Mac, the gear should open the panel too, even though the settings are already visible beside the tuner.
