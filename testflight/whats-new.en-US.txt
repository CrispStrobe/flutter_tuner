2.5.0 (build 11)

The microphone control now stays in the top-right corner. On iPad, settings sit below it in the right column. On iPhone, tap the gear to jump directly to settings.

What to test

• Start and stop tuning from the top-right microphone button, including after scrolling down.
• On iPad, rotate between portrait and landscape and check that the settings and tuner are easy to reach.
• On iPhone, use the gear to jump to settings. Change the instrument, tuning or A4 reference pitch, then start tuning again.
• On iPad, enable chord transcription and check that detected notes appear underneath the charts.
